Family Planning & Action Checklist

🤿 Snorkel Equipment

Pack your own well-fitting masks, snorkels, and fins from Australia for all 5 people (especially for the 9-year-old child). Water taxis and remote beaches do not have rental kiosks.

☀️ Sun Protection

Bring long-sleeve UV50+ rash vests for everyone. The tropical sun on the lagoon is intense, and rashies protect both skin and delicate reef corals from chemical sunscreens.

💳 Currency & Payments

The local currency is the Pacific Franc (CFP/XPF), pegged to the Euro (1 AUD ≈ 72–74 XPF). Visa and Mastercard are accepted at hotels and supermarkets; carry ~15,000–25,000 XPF cash for small village stalls and water taxis.

🚨 Booking Deadlines

If choosing Option 2, book Air Calédonie domestic flights and island rental cars 3–4 months ahead due to the Wednesday 11 Nov Armistice Day holiday.
