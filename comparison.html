🤿 Snorkel Equipment

Pack your own well-fitting masks, snorkels, and fins from Australia for all 5 people (especially for the 9-year-old child). Remote beaches lack rental kiosks.

☀️ UV Sun Protection

Bring long-sleeve UV50+ rash vests for everyone. The tropical sun on the lagoon is intense, and rashies protect both skin and delicate reef corals.

💳 Currency & Payments

Local currency is Pacific Franc (CFP/XPF), pegged to Euro (1 AUD ≈ 72–74 XPF). Carry ~20,000–30,000 XPF cash for small stalls and boat captains.

🚨 Booking Deadlines

If choosing Option 2, book Air Calédonie domestic flights and island rental cars 3–4 months ahead due to the Wednesday 11 Nov Armistice Day holiday.

🛒 Armistice Day Provisioning

Set a calendar reminder for Tuesday 10 Nov afternoon to buy food, snacks, and bottled water before all mainland supermarkets close on Wednesday.

🚗 Rental Car Reservation

Lock in a 7-seater SUV with airport terminal pickup/drop-off at La Tontouta for 5 passengers with 5 pieces of luggage.
