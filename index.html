Option 1 — Single Base Convenience

The Islet Explorer & Mainland Base

Base in Nouméa’s southern bays with private self-contained apartments. Spend your days hopping by speedboat to uninhabited marine sanctuaries, hiking ancient rainforests, and enjoying French bakeries.

📅 8 – 14 Nov 2026 (6 Nights)
👥 4 Adults + 1 Child (9 yo)
📍 Base: Baie des Citrons / Anse Vata
🧳 Pack/Unpack: Once Only

Overview & Family Suitability

This itinerary delivers maximum flexibility and zero accommodation relocations. Mainland Nouméa features safe, shark-netted swimming directly across from your apartment at Baie des Citrons, while world-class coral reef snorkeling is accessed via quick 5-to-35-minute water-taxi trips to protected outer marine reserves.

Family Pros: No unpacking and repacking mid-trip. The 9-year-old has calm, netted beach swimming every afternoon right outside your doorstep, combined with thrilling speedboat hops to snorkel with sea turtles.
Public Holiday Note (Wed 11 Nov — Armistice Day): Supermarkets and downtown stores close in Nouméa, but natural provincial parks and boat charters operate. Day 4 is specifically planned for the wilderness of Parc de la Rivière Bleue.

Detailed Day-by-Day Schedule

Day 1: Sunday, 8 Nov — Arrival & Settling In
Arrival Day
☀️ Afternoon (12:35 PM – 4:00 PM)

Land at La Tontouta International Airport (QF91 at 12:35 PM). Clear customs, collect your 7-seater rental vehicle at the terminal counter, and take the scenic 45-minute drive south along Route Territoriale 1 (RT1) to Nouméa. Check into your 2-bedroom oceanfront apartment at Baie des Citrons.

🌊 Late Afternoon (4:30 PM – 6:30 PM)

Walk across the street to the calm, shark-netted swimming zone at Baie des Citrons for a post-flight swim. Let the 9-year-old stretch their legs while adults enjoy iced coffee on the beach.

🌙 Evening (7:00 PM)

Casual waterfront dinner at Le Bilboquet Plage or L'Olympe overlooking the bay. Pick up initial milk, water, and pantry staples from Alimentation Michel-Ange (open 7 days until 8:00 PM).

Day 2: Monday, 9 Nov — Duck Island Snorkeling Trail & Ouen Toro
Marine Reserve
☀️ Morning (8:30 AM – 12:30 PM)

Fresh croissant run to L'Atelier Gourmand. Stroll to Anse Vata beach and take a 5-minute water taxi with Plage Loisirs out to Îlot Canard (Duck Island). Follow the underwater educational trail (sentier sous-marin) with numbered buoys identifying staghorn corals, clownfish, and sea anemones.

🌴 Afternoon (1:00 PM – 4:30 PM)

Water taxi return to Anse Vata. Casual French lunch at Le Roof or grab savory paninis. Drive 5 minutes up to Ouen Toro lookout for 360-degree views of the southern barrier reef. Visit the Aquarium des Lagons to see the fluorescent corals and sea turtles.

🌙 Evening

Home-cooked dinner in your apartment kitchen with sunset drinks from your private balcony.

Day 3: Tuesday, 10 Nov — Îlot Signal Turtle Sanctuary
Wildlife Highlight
☀️ Morning (8:45 AM – 1:30 PM)

Board your pre-booked speedboat taxi with Nautica Taxi-Boat at Port Moselle (pontoon K). Enjoy a 35-minute cruise across the turquoise lagoon to Îlot Signal. Snorkel along the reef drop-off where green turtles (Chelonia mydas) and eagle rays feed in shallow, clear water.

🌿 Afternoon (1:30 PM – 4:00 PM)

Walk the 1.6 km shaded perimeter trail around the island to observe nesting seabirds and the 1883 stone signal beacon. Speedboat returns to Port Moselle by 3:30 PM.

🛒 Crucial Evening Prep

Armistice Day Pre-Stocking: Stop at Super U Magenta or Carrefour Market to buy all groceries and supplies for Wednesday, when all mainland supermarkets close for the public holiday.

Day 4: Wednesday, 11 Nov (Armistice Day) — Rivière Bleue Wilderness
Rainforest Adventure
☀️ Morning (7:30 AM – 12:30 PM)

Pack a gourmet picnic with Tuesday's provisions. Drive 1h15 into the Grand Sud to Parc Provincial de la Rivière Bleue (open Wednesday through Sunday from 7:00 AM). Rent mountain bikes or take the park shuttle to spot New Caledonia’s national bird—the rare, flightless Kagu (Cagou).

🛶 Afternoon (1:00 PM – 4:00 PM)

Visit the ancient Grand Kaori tree (over 1,000 years old). Rent kayaks to glide through the eerie, mystical Forêt Noyée (Drowned Forest) on the artificial lake. Swim in the natural freshwater river pools before driving back to Nouméa.

🌙 Evening

Relaxed dinner at Baie des Citrons. The child can have an early night after an active day outdoors.

Day 5: Thursday, 12 Nov — Phare Amédée (Lighthouse & Reef Day)
Iconic Landmark
☀️ All-Day Excursion (8:30 AM – 4:30 PM)

Board the Mary D catamaran from Port Moselle out to the famous Amédée Lighthouse Island. Climb the 247 cast-iron spiral steps for breathtaking views across the barrier reef pass. Join the glass-bottom boat tour to view giant clam beds and banded sea kraits (tricot rayé). Snorkel the outer coral gardens.

🌙 Evening

Dinner at La Marmite du Pêcheur or Le Bout du Monde overlooking the yachts at Port Moselle Marina.

Day 6: Friday, 13 Nov — Market & Stand-Up Paddleboarding
Local Culture
☀️ Morning (7:00 AM – 11:30 AM)

Visit the vibrant hexagonal pavilions of the Marché de Port Moselle. Sample tropical papaya, passionfruit, fresh coconut water, and browse artisanal Kanak woodcarvings and vanilla pods.

🏄 Afternoon (1:00 PM – 4:30 PM)

Rent stand-up paddleboards or kayaks within the protected waters of Baie des Citrons. Optional afternoon scuba dive for certified adults with Abyss Plongée at the Boulari Barrier Reef Pass.

🌙 Evening

Celebration farewell dinner at L'Échappée Belle or Chez Toto in the historic Quartier Latin.

Day 7: Saturday, 14 Nov — Bakery Breakfast & Departure
Flight Home
☀️ Morning (8:00 AM – 11:00 AM)

Final morning bakery stop at L’Atelier Gourmand for fresh baguettes, chouquettes, and coffee. Pack suitcases and check out of the apartment by 10:30 AM.

🛫 Midday Departure

Drive 45 minutes north along the RT1 to La Tontouta International Airport. Return your rental car by 11:45 AM. Check in for QF92 departing at 1:50 PM for Sydney.

Snorkeling Intel

  • Îlot Signal: Top spot for green turtles, spotted eagle rays, and vibrant drop-offs (4–8m visibility).
  • Duck Island: Great shallow coral trail for the 9yo; marked buoys with clownfish and sea anemones.
  • Phare Amédée: Giant clams, banded sea kraits (tricot rayé), and coral gardens.
  • Baie des Citrons (Mainland): Safe netted swimming zone; sandy bottom with occasional goatfish and juvenile rays.

Food & Bakery Strategy

Top Bakeries:

  • L'Atelier Gourmand (Anse Vata): Artisanal croissants, pain au chocolat, sourdough baguettes.
  • Boulangerie du Faubourg: Authentic traditional French baguettes.

Recommended Dinners:

  • Le Bilboquet Plage: Family-friendly lagoon seafood and stone-baked pizzas.
  • Le Bout du Monde: Casual marina-side tapas and grilled local mahi-mahi.
