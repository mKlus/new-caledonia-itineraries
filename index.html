The Islet Explorer & Mainland Base

Base in Nouméa’s southern bays with private self-contained oceanfront apartments. Enjoy safe shark-netted beach swimming across the street, morning French bakeries, and high-speed boat hops to uninhabited marine sanctuaries.

Overview & Family Suitability

Zero Hotel Relocations

This itinerary delivers maximum family convenience and zero packing fatigue. Mainland Nouméa features calm, shark-netted swimming directly across the road from your apartment at Baie des Citrons, while pristine coral reefs are reached via effortless 5-to-35-minute water-taxi trips to protected offshore marine reserves.

🗺️ Geographic Footprint Context (South Province): Option 1 focuses on the Southern Lagoon & Grand Sud Rainforest (~70 km radius from Nouméa). Total driving over 6 days is only ~160 km (approx. 25 min/day). While Grande Terre extends 400 km to the north, concentrating on the south gives your family the highest ratio of active swimming, snorkeling, and relaxing time with zero packing stress.
Family Advantage for the 9-Year-Old: Unpack your bags once. The 9-year-old enjoys calm, protected beach swimming every afternoon right across the street, alongside exhilarating speedboat rides to snorkel with wild green sea turtles at Îlot Signal.
Public Holiday Note (Wed 11 Nov — Armistice Day): Mainland supermarkets and banks close in Nouméa, but natural provincial parks and boat charters operate normally. Day 4 is specifically planned for the grand wilderness and kayak trails of Parc de la Rivière Bleue.

Detailed Day-by-Day Schedule

DAY 1
Sunday, 8 Nov — Arrival & Settle In
Arrival ▲
🛬 Afternoon Arrival 12:35 PM – 4:00 PM

Touch down at La Tontouta International Airport (QF91 arrives at 12:35 PM). Clear customs, collect your pre-booked 7-seater SUV at the terminal counter, and take the scenic 45-minute drive south along Route Territoriale 1 (RT1) to Nouméa. Check into your 2-bedroom oceanfront apartment at Baie des Citrons.

🏊 Late Afternoon Swim 4:30 PM – 6:30 PM

Cross the street to the calm, shark-netted swimming zone at Baie des Citrons. Perfect for shedding travel fatigue and letting the 9-year-old splash in calm lagoon water while adults relax with iced café au lait.

🍽️ Sunset Dinner & Pantry Run 7:00 PM

Casual waterfront dinner at Le Bilboquet Plage or L'Olympe overlooking the bay. Pick up initial milk, bottled water, and pantry staples from Alimentation Michel-Ange (open 7 days until 8:00 PM).

DAY 2
Monday, 9 Nov — Duck Island Snorkeling Trail & Ouen Toro
Marine Reserve ▲
🦆 Morning Water Taxi to Duck Island 8:30 AM – 12:30 PM

Fresh croissant run to L'Atelier Gourmand. Stroll to Anse Vata beach and board a 5-minute water taxi with Plage Loisirs out to Îlot Canard (Duck Island). Swim along the underwater educational trail (sentier sous-marin) with numbered buoys identifying staghorn corals, clownfish, and sea anemones.

🔭 Panoramic Lookout & Aquarium 1:30 PM – 4:30 PM

Boat return to Anse Vata. Casual French lunch at Le Roof or grab savory baguettes. Drive 5 minutes up to Ouen Toro lookout for 360-degree views over the southern barrier reef. Visit the Aquarium des Lagons to see the fluorescent corals, nautilus, and sea turtles up close.

🌅 Balcony Sunset & Dinner 6:30 PM

Cook a relaxed dinner in your apartment kitchen with sunset drinks from your private sea-view balcony.

DAY 3
Tuesday, 10 Nov — Îlot Signal Turtle Sanctuary
Wildlife Highlight ▲
🐢 Speedboat to Îlot Signal 8:45 AM – 1:30 PM

Board your pre-booked speedboat taxi with Nautica Taxi-Boat at Port Moselle (pontoon K). Enjoy a thrilling 35-minute cruise across turquoise waters to Îlot Signal. Snorkel along the reef drop-off where wild green turtles (Chelonia mydas) and spotted eagle rays feed in shallow, clear waters.

🌿 Nature Trail & Seabird Colony 1:30 PM – 4:00 PM

Walk the 1.6 km shaded perimeter trail around the island to observe nesting seabirds and the 1883 stone signal beacon. Speedboat returns to Port Moselle by 3:30 PM.

🛒 Armistice Day Pre-Stocking 4:30 PM

Crucial Public Holiday Prep: Stop at Super U Magenta or Carrefour Market to buy all groceries and picnic provisions for Wednesday, when all mainland supermarkets close for the Armistice Day holiday.

DAY 4
Wednesday, 11 Nov (Public Holiday) — Rivière Bleue Wilderness
Armistice Day ▲
🌲 Ancient Rainforest Drive & Cagou Birds 7:30 AM – 12:30 PM

Pack a gourmet picnic with Tuesday's provisions. Drive 1h15 into the Grand Sud to Parc Provincial de la Rivière Bleue (open Wednesday through Sunday from 7:00 AM). Rent mountain bikes or take the park shuttle to spot New Caledonia’s national bird—the rare, flightless Kagu (Cagou).

🛶 Drowned Forest Kayak & Grand Kaori 1:00 PM – 4:00 PM

Visit the ancient Grand Kaori tree (over 1,000 years old). Rent kayaks to paddle through the eerie, mystical Forêt Noyée (Drowned Forest) on the lake. Swim in the natural freshwater river pools before driving back to Nouméa.

🍕 Bay Dining 7:00 PM

Relaxed dinner at Baie des Citrons. An early night for the family after a big day in the outdoors.

DAY 5
Thursday, 12 Nov — Phare Amédée Lighthouse & Outer Reef
Iconic Day Trip ▲
🗼 All-Day Marine Excursion 8:30 AM – 4:30 PM

Board the Mary D catamaran from Port Moselle out to the famous Amédée Lighthouse Island. Climb the 247 cast-iron spiral steps for breathtaking views across the barrier reef pass. Join the glass-bottom boat tour to view giant clam beds and banded sea kraits (tricot rayé). Snorkel the outer coral gardens.

⚓ Marina Seafood Dinner 7:00 PM

Dinner at La Marmite du Pêcheur or Le Bout du Monde overlooking the yachts at Port Moselle Marina.

DAY 6
Friday, 13 Nov — Market & Stand-Up Paddleboarding
Local Culture ▲
🍍 Morning Port Moselle Market 7:30 AM – 11:30 AM

Explore the hexagonal pavilions of the Marché de Port Moselle. Sample tropical papaya, passionfruit, fresh coconut water, and browse artisanal Kanak woodcarvings and vanilla pods.

🏄 Afternoon SUP & Beach Lounging 1:00 PM – 4:30 PM

Rent stand-up paddleboards or kayaks within the protected waters of Baie des Citrons. Optional afternoon scuba dive for certified adults with Abyss Plongée at the Boulari Barrier Reef Pass.

🍷 Celebration Farewell Dinner 7:00 PM

Celebration farewell dinner at L'Échappée Belle or Chez Toto in the historic Quartier Latin.

DAY 7
Saturday, 14 Nov — Bakery Morning & Flight Home
Departure ▲
🥐 Croissants & Checkout 8:00 AM – 10:30 AM

Final morning bakery stop at L’Atelier Gourmand for hot baguettes, chouquettes, and espresso. Pack suitcases and check out of the apartment by 10:30 AM.

🛫 Airport Drive & Departure 10:45 AM – 1:50 PM

Drive 45 minutes north along the RT1 to La Tontouta International Airport. Return your rental car by 11:45 AM. Check in for QF92 departing at 1:50 PM for Sydney.

Snorkeling Intel

Wildlife
  • Îlot Signal: Top spot for wild green sea turtles, spotted eagle rays, and vibrant coral drop-offs (4–8m visibility).
  • Duck Island: Superb shallow coral trail for the 9-year-old child; marked buoys with clownfish and sea anemones.
  • Phare Amédée: Giant clam beds, banded sea kraits (tricot rayé), and coral gardens.
  • Baie des Citrons: Safe netted swimming zone; sandy bottom with occasional goatfish and juvenile rays.

Food & Bakery Strategy

French Cuisine

🥖 Morning Bakeries:

  • L'Atelier Gourmand (Anse Vata): Artisanal croissants, pain au chocolat, sourdough baguettes.
  • Boulangerie du Faubourg: Traditional French baguettes and brioche.

🍷 Recommended Dinners:

  • Le Bilboquet Plage: Family-friendly lagoon seafood and stone-baked pizzas.
  • Le Bout du Monde: Casual marina-side tapas and grilled local mahi-mahi.
