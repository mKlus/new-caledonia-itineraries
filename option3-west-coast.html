🚙 Option 3 — Coastal Road Trip & Bush Heritage

The West Coast Reef & Bush Road Trip

Drive north along scenic Route Territoriale 1 (RT1) to the UNESCO World Heritage barrier reef lagoon at Poé Beach. Snorkel the green turtle sanctuary at the Shark Fault, hike the dramatic Three Bays pine cliff trail, and experience authentic Caldoche bush country.

📅 8 – 14 Nov 2026 (6 Nights)
👥 4 Adults + 1 Child (9 yo)
🚗 Total Self-Drive Freedom (RT1)
🏡 3N Poé Beach + 3N Nouméa

Overview & Family Suitability

Road Trip Adventure

This itinerary completely avoids domestic flight check-ins and strict luggage weight limits while providing total independence. Because La Tontouta International Airport is situated 50 km north of Nouméa, you land, collect your 7-seater SUV, and head directly north to Bourail/Poé (a smooth 1h45m drive on sealed highway), saving backtracking on Day 1.

🗺️ Geographic Footprint Context (West Coast & Central Grande Terre): Option 3 covers over 40% of the entire 400 km length of Grande Terre, reaching up to Bourail and Domaine de Deva (170 km north of Nouméa). Total driving over 6 days is ~380 km (approx. 50 min/day). Landing at La Tontouta and heading directly north saves 50 km of highway driving. This delivers wide-open barrier reef lagoons, pine cliff ocean lookouts, and outback heritage while keeping car travel comfortable for a 9-year-old child.
Unique Geographic Advantage: Poé Beach features 17 kilometers of continuous, shallow white-sand lagoon protected by the UNESCO barrier reef and shaded by ironwood trees. The barrier reef is easily reached by local glass-bottom boats to snorkel with sea turtles in crystal-clear water.
Active Adventures for the 9-Year-Old: Stand-up paddleboarding in the flat, calm lagoon right outside your chalet, spotting surfacing sea turtles at Turtle Bay from pine-fringed coastal cliffs, and riding mountain bikes along the wide trails of Domaine de Deva.

Detailed Day-by-Day Schedule

DAY 1
Sunday, 8 Nov — Direct Drive North to Poé Beach
Road Trip Start ▲
🚗 Pick Up SUV & Scenic Drive North 12:35 PM – 3:30 PM

Touch down at La Tontouta Airport (QF91 arrives at 12:35 PM). Pick up your 7-seater SUV from the terminal counter. Head directly north on Route Territoriale 1 (RT1) through the rolling green hills and niaouli savannah of the West Coast. Stop in Bourail town to pick up evening essentials, then continue 15 minutes to Poé Beach. Check into your beachfront chalet.

🌊 Warm Sunset Lagoon Swim 4:00 PM – 6:30 PM

Step directly onto the soft white sand of Poé Beach. The water is warm, shallow, and completely wave-free inside the lagoon barrier. Enjoy a sunset beach swim.

🍽️ Veranda Welcome Dinner 7:00 PM

Casual dinner on the beach at L'Alizé restaurant or enjoy a self-catered meal on your private veranda listening to the breeze in the ironwood trees.

DAY 2
Monday, 9 Nov — Glass-Bottom Boat & Shark Fault Snorkel
Reef Highlight ▲
🐢 Barrier Reef & Shark Fault Snorkel 8:30 AM – 12:30 PM

Board the Bateau Taxi Nautile or local glass-bottom boat directly from Poé Beach. Cruise across the turquoise lagoon to the outer barrier reef and the famous Shark Fault (La Faille aux Requins). Snorkel in this protected deep underwater canyon where dozens of green sea turtles feed alongside harmless white-tip reef sharks, eagle rays, and giant trevally.

🏄 Afternoon SUP & Lagoon Paddling 1:30 PM – 5:00 PM

Rent stand-up paddleboards or double kayaks right off the sand at Poé. Paddle in the turquoise shallows. Relax under the shaded ironwood trees with cold drinks.

🍖 Bush Country Barbecue Night 6:30 PM

Fire up the outdoor barbecue at your chalet. Grill famous local Bourail beef steaks or wild venison (*cerf*) from Bourail’s local butchery, paired with fresh French bread.

DAY 3
Tuesday, 10 Nov — Three Bays Coastal Hike & Domaine de Deva
Scenic Nature ▲
🥾 Sentier des Trois Baies (Three Bays Hike) 8:00 AM – 11:30 AM

Hike the renowned Sentier des Trois Baies (Three Bays Trail). Start at landmark Roche Percée (Pierced Rock) and walk under the canopy of towering ancient columnar pine trees. Ascend to panoramic clifftops overlooking Turtle Bay (Baie des Tortues) and Lovers' Bay (Baie des Amoureux). Watch loggerhead turtles surfacing in the waves below.

🚵 Domaine de Deva Lookouts & Trails 1:00 PM – 4:30 PM

Head into the protected Domaine de Deva estate. Rent mountain bikes or take an easy walking track up to the Oua Koué lookout for sweeping views across the UNESCO-listed lagoon and barrier reef passes.

🛒 Armistice Day Pre-Stocking 5:00 PM

Stocking Up for Wednesday: Purchase groceries and breakfast supplies at Supermarché Match in Bourail before shops close for the Armistice Day holiday tomorrow.

DAY 4
Wednesday, 11 Nov (Public Holiday) — Fort Teremba & Drive South to Nouméa
Base Transition ▲
🏰 Historic Fort Teremba Exploration 9:00 AM – 12:00 PM

Check out of your Poé chalet. Drive 35 minutes south along RT1 to Fort Teremba in Moindou. Explore the restored 19th-century military fort and penal colony overlooking the turquoise Mara Bay. Great history learning for adults and the 9-year-old child.

🚗 Drive South to Nouméa 12:30 PM – 3:30 PM

Continue south along RT1 (1h30m). Arrive in Nouméa and check into your 2-bedroom oceanfront apartment at Baie des Citrons. Unpack for the remaining 3 nights.

🏊 Netted Sunset Swim & Bistro 4:30 PM – 7:30 PM

Late afternoon swim in the calm, shark-netted waters of Baie des Citrons. Dinner at Le Bilboquet Plage or L'Olympe.

DAY 5
Thursday, 12 Nov — Îlot Signal Turtle Sanctuary Speedboat
Marine Reserve ▲
🐢 Speedboat to Îlot Signal 8:45 AM – 2:00 PM

Board your pre-booked speedboat taxi from Port Moselle. Cruise 35 minutes out to Îlot Signal. Snorkel along the reef wall where green sea turtles and eagle rays feed in clear water. Walk the 1.6 km island trail around the seabird colony.

🔭 Ouen Toro Lookout & Gelato 3:00 PM – 5:30 PM

Return to mainland Nouméa. Drive up to Ouen Toro for barrier reef views, followed by artisan Italian gelato at Amorino at Baie des Citrons.

🍷 Latin Quarter Dinner 7:00 PM

Dinner in Nouméa at Chez Toto in the Latin Quarter.

DAY 6
Friday, 13 Nov — Duck Island Snorkel, Market & Culture
Local Culture ▲
🦆 Duck Island & Port Moselle Market 8:00 AM – 12:30 PM

Visit the vibrant morning Marché de Port Moselle for tropical fruit and crafts. Take the 5-minute water taxi to Îlot Canard (Duck Island) to swim the underwater coral trail with the 9-year-old.

🏛️ Tjibaou Cultural Centre 2:00 PM – 4:30 PM

Visit the famous Tjibaou Cultural Centre to experience Kanak history, art, and architectural pavilions designed by Renzo Piano.

🌅 Farewell Dinner 7:00 PM

Farewell celebration dinner at L'Échappée Belle overlooking the bay.

DAY 7
Saturday, 14 Nov — Bakery Breakfast & Departure
Departure ▲
🥐 Morning Croissants & Packing 8:00 AM – 10:30 AM

Final morning croissants and coffee at L’Atelier Gourmand. Pack suitcases and check out by 10:30 AM.

🛫 Airport Drive & Departure 10:45 AM – 1:50 PM

Drive 45 minutes north along the RT1 to La Tontouta International Airport. Return your rental car by 11:45 AM. Check in for QF92 departing at 1:50 PM for Sydney.

Snorkeling Intel

West Coast
  • The Shark Fault (Faille aux Requins): Underwater canyon cutting through the barrier reef where green turtles and harmless reef sharks gather.
  • Poé Lagoon: 17 km of shallow, wave-free sandy lagoon. Ideal for paddling, SUP, and child snorkeling.
  • Îlot Signal (Nouméa): Snorkel with wild sea turtles on Day 5 during the Nouméa mainland stay.
