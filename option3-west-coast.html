Option 3 — Coastal Road Trip & Bush Heritage

The West Coast Reef & Bush Road Trip

Drive north along the scenic Route Territoriale 1 (RT1) to the UNESCO World Heritage barrier reef lagoon at Poé Beach. Snorkel the green turtle sanctuary at the Shark Fault, hike the dramatic Three Bays coastal trail, and experience authentic Caldoche bush country.

📅 8 – 14 Nov 2026 (6 Nights)
👥 4 Adults + 1 Child (9 yo)
🚗 Road Trip: RT1 Highway (Airport Pickup)
🏡 Bases: 3N Poé Beach + 3N Nouméa

Overview & Family Suitability

This itinerary eliminates domestic flight schedules while giving your family total self-drive freedom. Because La Tontouta Airport is already located 50 km north of Nouméa, you land and head directly north to Bourail/Poé (a smooth 1h45m drive on sealed highway), saving backtracking on Day 1.

Unique Geographic Advantage: Poé Beach features 17 kilometers of continuous, shallow white sand lagoon backed by tranquil ironwood trees. The barrier reef is easily reached by local glass-bottom boat taxis to snorkel with sea turtles in crystal-clear water.
Active Adventures for the 9-Year-Old: Stand-up paddleboarding in the flat, calm lagoon right in front of your chalet, spotting sea turtles at Turtle Bay from dramatic cliffs, and riding mountain bikes along the wide trails of Domaine de Deva.

Detailed Day-by-Day Schedule

Day 1: Sunday, 8 Nov — Direct Drive North to Poé Beach
Road Trip Start
🚗 Afternoon (12:35 PM – 3:30 PM)

Touch down at La Tontouta Airport (QF91 at 12:35 PM). Pick up your 7-seater SUV from the terminal counter. Head directly north on Route Territoriale 1 (RT1) through the rolling green hills and niaouli savannah of the West Coast. Stop in Bourail town to pick up evening essentials, then continue 15 minutes to Poé Beach. Check into your beachfront chalet.

🌊 Late Afternoon (4:00 PM – 6:30 PM)

Step directly onto the soft white sand of Poé Beach. The water is warm, shallow, and completely wave-free inside the lagoon barrier. Enjoy a sunset beach swim.

🌙 Evening

Casual dinner on the beach at L'Alizé restaurant or enjoy a self-catered meal on your private veranda listening to the breeze in the ironwood trees.

Day 2: Monday, 9 Nov — Glass-Bottom Boat & Shark Fault Snorkel
Reef Highlight
🐢 Morning (8:30 AM – 12:30 PM)

Board the Bateau Taxi Nautile or local glass-bottom boat directly from Poé Beach. Cruise across the turquoise lagoon to the outer barrier reef and the famous Shark Fault (La Faille aux Requins). Snorkel in this protected deep underwater canyon where dozens of green sea turtles feed alongside harmless white-tip reef sharks, eagle rays, and giant trevally.

🏄 Afternoon (1:30 PM – 5:00 PM)

Rent stand-up paddleboards or double kayaks right off the sand at Poé. Paddle in the turquoise shallows. Relax under the shaded ironwood trees with cold drinks.

🍖 Evening

Fire up the outdoor barbecue at your chalet. Grill famous local Bourail beef steaks or wild venison (*cerf*) from Bourail’s local butchery, paired with fresh French bread.

Day 3: Tuesday, 10 Nov — Three Bays Coastal Hike & Domaine de Deva
Scenic Nature
🥾 Morning (8:00 AM – 11:30 AM)

Hike the renowned Sentier des Trois Baies (Three Bays Trail). Start at the landmark Roche Percée (Pierced Rock) and walk under the canopy of towering ancient columnar pine trees. Ascend to panoramic clifftops overlooking Turtle Bay (Baie des Tortues) and Lovers' Bay (Baie des Amoureux). Watch loggerhead turtles surfacing in the turquoise waves below.

🚵 Afternoon (1:00 PM – 4:30 PM)

Head into the protected Domaine de Deva estate. Rent mountain bikes or take an easy walking track up to the Oua Koué lookout for sweeping views across the UNESCO-listed lagoon and barrier reef passes.

🛒 Evening (Holiday Prep)

Stocking Up for Wednesday: Purchase groceries and breakfast supplies at Supermarché Match in Bourail before shops close for the Armistice Day holiday tomorrow.

Day 4: Wednesday, 11 Nov (Armistice Day) — Scenic Drive South to Nouméa
Heritage Road Trip
🚗 Morning & Midday (9:30 AM – 1:30 PM)

Final morning beach stroll at Poé. Check out and drive south along RT1 (approx. 2 hours). Stop at the historic 19th-century penal colony fort of Fort Teremba in Moindou. Picnic lunch under the shady trees.

🌊 Afternoon (2:30 PM – 6:00 PM)

Arrive in Nouméa and check into your ocean-view apartment at Baie des Citrons. Put on your swimsuits and head straight to the safe, netted beach across the street for an afternoon swim and beach games.

🌙 Evening

Stroll the lively Baie des Citrons boardwalk for tapas and dinner at Le Bilboquet Plage or La Bodega del Mar.

Day 5: Thursday, 12 Nov — Speedboat to Îlot Signal Sanctuary
Marine Reserve
🐢 Morning & Early Afternoon (8:45 AM – 2:30 PM)

Board your pre-booked water taxi from Port Moselle to Îlot Signal. Spend the morning snorkeling with wild green sea turtles and eagle rays along the outer reef drop-off. Walk the shaded 1.6 km nature trail.

🌆 Late Afternoon & Evening

Return to Port Moselle. Drive up to the Ouen Toro lookout to watch kite-surfers in the sunset. Dinner at Le Bout du Monde marina bistro.

Day 6: Friday, 13 Nov — Duck Island & Cultural Nouméa
City & Marine
☀️ Morning (8:30 AM – 12:30 PM)

Take a 5-minute water taxi to Îlot Canard (Duck Island) for easy, shallow reef snorkeling along the marked underwater trail. Visit the open-air sculpture garden on the island.

🏛️ Afternoon (1:30 PM – 4:30 PM)

Visit the Marché de Port Moselle and explore the world-renowned Tchibaou Cultural Centre to learn about Kanak traditions and contemporary South Pacific art.

🌙 Evening

Celebration farewell dinner at L'Échappée Belle or an authentic French crêperie.

Day 7: Saturday, 14 Nov — Bakery Run & Terminal Return
Flight Home
☀️ Morning (8:00 AM – 10:45 AM)

Pick up fresh croissants and baguettes from L'Atelier Gourmand. Pack bags and check out of your Nouméa apartment by 10:30 AM.

🛫 Midday Departure

Drive 45 minutes north on RT1 back to La Tontouta International Airport. Return your 7-seater rental vehicle directly to the airport depot at 11:45 AM. Check in for QF92 departing at 1:50 PM.

Road Trip Driving Intel

  • Route: Route Territoriale 1 (RT1) is fully sealed, wide, and well signposted.
  • Drive Times: La Tontouta Airport to Poé Beach is approx. 135 km (1h45m – 2h). Poé to Nouméa is approx. 165 km (2h15m).
  • Vehicle: A 7-seater SUV (e.g. Mitsubishi Outlander / Peugeot 5008) comfortably accommodates 5 passengers plus all luggage.

Snorkeling Intel

  • La Faille aux Requins (Shark Fault): World Heritage marine reserve inside the outer reef pass; dense concentrations of green turtles and harmless reef sharks.
  • Turtle Bay (Baie des Tortues): High chance of seeing loggerhead turtles surfacing in the surf from the cliffs.
  • Poé Lagoon: Extremely calm, shallow water ideal for children to paddleboard and snorkel small coral bommies.
